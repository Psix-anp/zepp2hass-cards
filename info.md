# Zepp2Hass Cards

A suite of Home Assistant dashboard cards designed around the entities exposed by the community Zepp2Hass integration:

- Amazfit Overview Card
- Amazfit Sleep Card
- Amazfit Activity Card
- Amazfit Health Card
- Amazfit Training Card
- Family Activity Card

The first five cards provide registry-based Zepp2Hass entity discovery. Family Activity is intentionally generic and can rank arbitrary Home Assistant step sensors.

This is an independent community frontend project and is not an official Zepp, Amazfit, Home Assistant, or Zepp2Hass project.
